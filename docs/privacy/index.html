SpaceView Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · App: SpaceView (Android, package com.spaceview.app) · Contact: fableformlabs@gmail.com

SpaceView is an augmented-reality measuring app. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your measurements, photos and gallery stay on your phone. SpaceView has no account and never uploads them. Some Google services built into the app (ads, purchases and on-device image tools) may collect limited device and diagnostic data, as described below.

1. Information that stays on your phone

  • Camera. SpaceView uses the camera only while you measure, scan an object or place an item in your room. Camera frames are processed on the phone in real time and are not stored or sent anywhere, except the photo of an object you choose to save.
  • Measurements, History and Gallery. Measurements, saved object photos and cutouts, and their dimensions are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Object names and background removal. When you save an object, SpaceView may suggest a name and remove the photo's background. This is done on the phone by Google ML Kit. The photo itself is not uploaded.
  • Settings and calibration (units, sounds, depth, hands-free, calibration) are stored on the phone.

We (the developer of SpaceView) do not receive, collect or have access to any of this information. SpaceView has no sign-in, no user accounts and no servers of its own.

3. Your choices

  • Ad consent: where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads. You can change it anytime in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's Android settings (Privacy → Ads).
  • No ads: SpaceView Pro removes all ads.
  • Camera permission: you can revoke it in Android settings. Measuring needs the camera.

4. Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete my data in the app to permanently delete your History, Gallery (with its pictures and models), calibration and settings from your phone. Uninstalling the app also deletes them. Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing else for us to delete. Your Pro subscription is managed by Google Play: cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

5. Sharing

SpaceView only shares a measurement or a saved picture when you choose to share it (for example to WhatsApp) using Android's share menu. We do not sell or share your personal data.

6. Children

SpaceView is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Google services encrypt the data they collect in transit.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
