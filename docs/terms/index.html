1. What SpaceView does

SpaceView uses your phone's camera and augmented reality (Google ARCore) to estimate distances, heights and sizes, save pictures of objects at their measured size, and preview them in your room. It needs a phone that supports Google Play Services for AR.

2. Measurements are estimates

  • Every measurement is an estimate. The app shows its expected error as "±". The real error can be larger, for example in poor light, on plain or shiny surfaces, at long distances, on sloping ground, or if the phone moves too fast.
  • Do not rely on SpaceView where an error could cause injury, damage or financial loss: for example construction, engineering, legal or property boundaries, safety distances, navigation, medical use, or cutting materials. Check important measurements with a tape measure or another proper instrument.
  • "Fits / Tight / Too big" results and room previews are guides, not guarantees.
